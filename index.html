What We Do?

Darshan and Puja Timings

The temple opens its doors daily from 5:30 AM to 12:30 PM and reopens in the evening from 4:30 PM to 9:00 PM, providing devotees ample time to seek blessings. The day begins with the Mangala Aarti at 5:45 AM, followed by regular puja and darshan throughout the morning. In the evening, the temple hosts the serene Sandhya Aarti at 6:30 PM, creating a divine atmosphere. Special rituals, including Abhishekam and Homa, are conducted on full moon days and during festivals like Navratri and Diwali, attracting a large number of devotees. Devotees are encouraged to participate in these rituals and experience the spiritual grandeur of the temple.

Temple Events

The temple hosts a variety of spiritual and cultural events throughout the year, celebrating major festivals with grandeur and devotion. Key events include Navratri, where daily pujas and evening cultural programs are organized, and Diwali, marked by special rituals and vibrant decorations. During Mahashivratri, the temple remains open all night, offering devotees the opportunity to participate in Abhishekam and other sacred ceremonies. The Annual Rath Yatra is a major attraction, drawing devotees from far and wide to witness the procession of the deity’s chariot. Other regular events include Bhajan Sandhya (evening devotional singing), Satsangs, and community service activities. These events provide a space for spiritual growth and community bonding, welcoming all devotees to participate and seek blessings.

The temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva, revered as the embodiment of cosmic energy and transformation. The sanctum sanctorum houses a beautifully adorned Shivalinga, symbolizing Lord Shiva's infinite power and presence. Alongside, the temple also venerates Goddess Parvati, the divine consort of Shiva, representing love, fertility, and devotion. Devotees also offer prayers to Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles, and Lord Kartikeya, the god of wisdom and war. Each deity is enshrined in intricately carved sanctuaries, reflecting the spiritual and artistic heritage of the temple. Daily rituals and offerings, including Abhishekam (sacred bath), are performed to honor these deities, inviting devotees to seek their blessings for prosperity, peace, and spiritual fulfillment.
